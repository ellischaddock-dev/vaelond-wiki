# The Powers That Be

## Perfid

Perfid is locked in a perpetual **cold war with Doro**, both sides trading steel and spellfire over a harsh, blood-soaked stretch of contested land known as **the Spear and the Maw**—a narrow peninsula and canyon that neither side can afford to lose.

Within its borders, Perfid is caught in a fraught stalemate between church and state. The **[[Church of Elgira]]** holds vast spiritual and social power, while the government and military under **House d'Eamon** push for greater secular control.

Tensions rise as both factions become more extreme. The Church's efforts to expand its influence into Minir and Rakara are increasingly treated as political acts rather than simple missionary work.

## Doro

Doro wages war with Perfid by day, and with itself by night.

It is a realm of grand palaces, endless bureaucracy and veiled knives. Though powerful on paper, **Doro is paralysed by internal politics**. Rule is divided between **six Great Houses**, each competing through marriage pacts, scandal, trade and sabotage.

Those outside the corridors of power are often treated as thralls or collateral for the war. The people grow weary, but few dare break the ancient web of alliances holding the realm together.

See also: [[Doroan Faiths]].

## Minir

Minir remains outwardly **detached**, building its strength in quiet. Its universities, archives and arcane workshops have become the envy of the world. While others fight, Minir studies, trading with every side while committing fully to none.

The **Pale Seminary** is a university located in **Vehr**. Rumours persist of hidden projects beneath its halls, including research into ancient magic and Ardynian technology.

Minir refuses to join the war between Doro and Perfid, preferring to use their rivalry to secure favourable trade and political leverage.

## Rakara

Far to the south, **Rakara** stands on the edge of transformation.

Over centuries, dozens of tribes were gradually absorbed into the Five Clans. Ten years ago, **Anthea Bloodclot** began styling herself the **Lord of the Five Clans**. The office had never formally existed: Anthea drew upon an old myth and used the title to build a personal following, mediate disputes and encourage shared law and cooperation.

Her authority depended upon reputation, negotiation and the willingness of the clans to participate. It was not a recognised crown or permanent constitutional office.

One year ago, Anthea was murdered near [[Somnolent]]. Voruun now publicly claims to be her rightful successor, with Khaali the only clan offering open support. The others reject, avoid or carefully qualify the claim. Rakara remains fractured, and old rivalries are returning beneath the language of unity.
