# Doroan Faiths

Doro shares several gods with [[01 Welcome to Vaelond/Religion/Rakaran Faiths|Rakara]], but interprets them through its own culture of authority, reputation, contracts and noble obligation.

> [!note] Shared gods
> The symbol **∑** marks a god shared with Rakara.

## Ulvarn Ascendant ∑

*Power, Ambition, Rule, War*

Once a steppe god of conquest, Ulvarn in Doro is worshipped as the **legitimiser of authority**. He does not care *how* power is taken—only that it is held.

- **Doctrine:** Power unused is power wasted.
- **Tension:** Orvak and Rakaran traditionalists see this as a corruption of Ulvarn's trial-by-strength ethos.
- **Common rite:** A noble swears a private oath to Ulvarn before accepting office.

### Prayer, spoken privately

> *“Ulvarn, set me above those who doubt me.”*

## Verei of the Gilded Mask ∑

*Art, Rhetoric, Performance, Lies*

Verei is **controlled deception**—the god of speeches, fashion, ceremony and lies that sound like truth.

- **Doctrine:** Reality is what the audience believes.
- **Worship:** Plays, salons, political debates and curated scandal.
- **Symbol:** A polished mask with no cracks.

### Prayer before court

> *“Verei, let them see only what I choose to show.”*

## Talik of the Balanced Ledger ∑

*Trade, Contracts, Luck*

Talik survives in Doro as a god of **contracts, margins and loopholes**, rather than chaos.

- **Doctrine:** A deal is sacred—interpretation is flexible.
- **Worship sites:** Counting houses, tax halls and gambling rooms.
- **Common view:** Many swear by Talik publicly, but curse him privately.

### Prayer written in margins

> *“Talik, let the numbers favour me.”*

## Ezaa the Soft Whisper ∑

*Influence, Secrets, Diplomacy*

Ezaa's winds are quieter in Doro—felt in corridors, not open skies.

- **Doctrine:** Words shape nations more surely than armies.
- **Followers:** Envoys, spies, blackmailers and letter-writers.
- **Rite:** Burning unread letters to “return secrets to the wind”.

### Prayer

> *“Ezaa, carry this truth where I cannot.”*

## Domara the Matron Unbroken

*Family, Lineage, Duty, Marriage*

Domara is the **spine of Doroan society**. She sanctifies bloodlines, arranged marriages and the continuation of the house above the individual.

- **Doctrine:** The family endures; the self is temporary.
- **Worship:** Births, betrothals and inheritance rites.
- **Heresy:** Choosing love over obligation.

### Prayer spoken at weddings

> *“Domara, bind us tighter than affection ever could.”*

## Cassivar the Oath-Bound

*Honour, Law, Reputation*

Cassivar governs **public honour**, not private morality. What matters is what can be proven, sworn or witnessed.

- **Doctrine:** An unbroken oath is worth more than truth.
- **Worshippers:** Judges, advocates and duellists.
- **Ritual:** Swearing before steel or ink.

### Prayer

> *“Cassivar, let my name survive the telling.”*

## Veltrin the Veiled Hand

*Assassination, Silence, Necessary Evil*

Veltrin is never worshipped openly. His shrines are hidden and his priests unnamed.

- **Doctrine:** Some deaths preserve order.
- **Followers:** Intelligence circles and sanctioned killers.
- **Public stance:** Officially denied by all houses.

### Prayer, never written

> *“Veltrin, let this end quietly.”*

## Rhozka, She Who Records the End ∑

*Death, Legacy, Memory*

Rhozka is shared with Rakara but interpreted differently: not as a gentle guide, but as **the archivist of consequence**.

- **Doctrine:** Death is final—but reputation is not.
- **Worship:** Funerals, sealed wills and historical records.
- **Fear:** Rhozka remembers what others erase.

### Prayer

> *“Rhozka, remember me as I intend to be remembered.”*
