# Religion

Though wizards work wonders with their Art and adventurers take their fates into their own hands, it is on the gods that most folk in Vaelond depend when they have need.

The gods play a role in the lives of nearly everyone, from the mightiest lord to the meanest urchin. Most people worship many gods throughout their lives, depending on their needs, work and family. Worship is deeply practical: people make offerings, say prayers or keep small tokens of the gods they feel closest to, not out of dogma, but to earn favour or avoid misfortune.

Although worship is common across Vaelond, the visage of the gods varies from region to region. Each pantheon reflects the culture that shaped it—from monolithic Perfid and its Church of Elgira to the disparate and unruly collection of faiths found throughout Rakara.

## Regional traditions

- [[Rakaran Faiths]] — a varied and practical pantheon shaped by the Five Clans
- [[Doroan Faiths]] — gods reframed through authority, reputation, contracts and noble society
- [[Perfidian Faith]] — the exclusive worship of Elgira and veneration of her four saints
