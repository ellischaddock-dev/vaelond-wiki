# Time and Calendars

A week in Vaelond lasts **eight days**. Some days have traditional purposes attached to them, although customs vary by region.

## Days of the week

| Day | Traditional association |
|---|---|
| **Onesday** | Worship and rest |
| **Twosday** | — |
| **Thirdsday** | — |
| **Fourthsday** | — |
| **Fifthsday** | — |
| **Sixthday** | Remembrance and funerals |
| **Seventhday** | — |
| **Eighthday** | Family and feasts |

## The year

Each month consists of five full weeks, mirroring the moon's cycle. There are ten months in a year, making a Vaelondian year **400 days** long.

| Month | Seasonal character |
|---|---|
| **Frostmelt** | Winter's thaw; the new year begins |
| **Thawen** | First blooms and lambing season |
| **Greengold** | Spring in full; trade resumes |
| **Blazereach** | Dry heat begins; fire rituals |
| **Highsun** | Peak of summer; war campaigns |
| **Duskhail** | Fading light; storms gather |
| **Fallshade** | Leaves turn; long hunts begin |
| **Stonecrack** | Frost returns; ancestors are honoured |
| **Whitesky** | Snows rise; spirits walk |
| **Deeprest** | End of year; sacred stillness |
