# Session 06: Smoke, Song and Secrets

> [!abstract] Session summary
> Back in Somnolent, each adventurer follows a separate lead involving the Pike, Ardynia and Broken Fang contraband.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 6 |
| **Recorded** | 23 October 2025 at 20:15 |
| **Arc** | Somnolent investigations |

## 4AM – The Walk of Shame

The Squishy Boys stumbled back into [[Somnolent]] under the cloak of predawn silence — bloodied, burned, and barely upright. Their return did not go unnoticed. Deputy Gayle Bramble was waiting. With arms crossed and eyes sharp, she dressed down Terrick for his impromptu adventure, making it clear that his wife, Rowena, had been worried sick. The ranger sheepishly scurried home to whatever frosty reception awaited him.

Gayle passed on one final message: Romayne Drayton expected a full report that evening.

The group scattered, agreeing on a 1PM rendezvous at the Drowsy Coin. Ballar suggested rest.

Ballar, of course, ignored his own advice.

## 4:30AM – Ballar Gets a Step Ahead

Taking a moment to make sure he was perfumed to perfection, Ballar made his way to the [[The Powers That Be#Doro|Doro]] Embassy. Inside the cold marble halls, he was met by Stacii, Ambassador Ouin Brond's ever-prickly assistant. After some theatrical insistence, she begrudgingly let him through.

Ouin was pensive, glass of wine in hand, uninterested at first — until Ballar began talking. He revealed everything: the Bromere coin, the [[Rootbound]], Anthea Bloodclot’s assassination, and more. Ouin dismissed any direct Bromere involvement in the abduction attempt, but pointed Ballar toward a potential link — Ervain “The Pike,” a former Bromere commander frequenting a dice game at the One Ball Inn, run by Alaaric Hammerfall.

Ouin encouraged caution, asking Ballar to keep his findings quiet until they could be exploited. She reminded him that secrets were a currency of his family and challenged him to keep his unpredictable companions in check. Ballar nodded… somewhat evasively.

Before leaving, Ballar leveraged Ouin's goodwill to press for more on the Broken Fang, the mercenary group n’Dong had mentioned. She was only too eager to point them out on the map, confirming they had disrupted Doro trade routes and promising to provide more intelligence.

Mission complete, Ballar headed to the gym to get a pump on, followed by a well-earned trance in the sauna.

## 8AM – Stars, Smoke and Ashes

Elsewhere, Erelmor tranced in the quiet solitude of Dustmere Grounds, but found no peace. A song crept into his thoughts, unbidden and sinister — a melody from the Maw, tied to the darkest day of his life. His trance spiralled into a vivid memory: smoke, ash, his home in ruin.

The song was real.

He awoke to hear it drifting from a drunken trader. Without hesitation, Erelmor tackled the man into the bushes, blade to throat. Desperate, panicked, the man revealed he was a former House Daskel soldier. He’d heard the tune earlier that morning—someone had sung it at a dice game in the One Ball Inn to taunt a man named Ervain.

Almost sated, Erelmor rifled through the man’s belongings, claiming a handful of coins and a dram of whiskey, warning him never to sing of The Pike again.

Consumed by rage, Erelmor charged to the One Ball Inn. Finding it closed, he fought the urge to break in, instead wandering toward the arcade, simmering, waiting.

Meanwhile, Ieuan awoke in quiet contemplation, only to find Father Zantos waiting. The two spoke at length. Zantos praised Ieuan’s choice to walk the Circle of Stars, linking it to Orun the Verdant Kin. Their conversation turned to the [[The Sundering of Ardynia|Ardynians]], with Zantos expressing worry at how recklessly the party had disturbed the ruins. He shared his upbringing in [[Clan Erzhin]], where ancient truths about the Ardynians were kept in the Annals of Stone—knowledge forgotten elsewhere in [[Rakara]].

Talk shifted to the Rootbound. Though both men were guarded, Ieuan learned that Zantos once called himself a member, before withdrawing when the group turned too rebellious, even murderous. Zantos was heartbroken to hear they might now be aligned with Elgira’s Church and [[The Powers That Be#Perfid|Perfid]].

As they parted, Zantos offered a gift: a Star Seed, promising it would bloom once Ieuan fully embraced his druidic path.

## 10AM – Making a Mark

Refreshed, dressed in finery and fully attuned to his Ring of Mind Shielding, Ballar tried to claim reimbursement from Stacii, but was met with the usual Doro bureaucracy. Undeterred, he marched to the Silent Court and was finally shown to Alaaric’s office, where the treasurer unlocked a complicated safe and handed over the Anthea Bloodclot death report.

Not done yet, Ballar leaned into his next objective—infiltrating the dice game. Playing the role of curious newcomer, he managed to catch Alaaric’s interest. The price of admission: 250 gold, plus 25 gold per companion watching. Ballar, ever the opportunist, now had one foot firmly inside Somnolent’s underbelly.

## 12PM – Alcohol-Fueled Escapades

n’Dong woke in the woods, sun dappling through the trees. After a failed attempt at river fishing, he heard a racket from upriver. A man floated by on a makeshift barrel-raft, clearly up to no good.

n’Dong sprinted ahead, reaching the bridge just as the raft passed beneath. Without hesitation, he leapt—crashing onto the raft with a thud that nearly capsized it. A quick Suggestion spell convinced the smuggler to dock the raft and turn over the goods, but as they reached shore, n’Dong slipped, face-planting in the shallows.

The man tried to flee, only to be snagged by n’Dong’s clawed grip. During the interrogation, the man admitted he was just the delivery boy. He’d make the drop… and someone else would take the loot. That was all n’Dong needed.

Volunteering himself as cargo, n’Dong climbed into one of the whiskey barrels. The journey was long, wet, and deeply uncomfortable—but he stayed the course.

Meanwhile, Enrak woke in a Drowsy Coin filled with drunken song. At the centre was Ersil Loquella, bard and rascal, leading a rousing tavern anthem about Enrak the Brazen Flame. Delighted, Enrak cast Firebolt into the hearth — accidentally scorching the bricks — and bought another round.

Between drinks, Ersil pressed for stories. Enrak spoke warmly of his companions: Ballar the Bold, n’Dong the Poisonous, Erelmor the Gold-Seeker, and Ieuan the Mystical. Though he rejected the notion of being the group's leader, he did share his ambition — to rise in [[Clan Khaali]], and uncover Anthea Bloodclot’s killer.

Ersil played him a different tune: *The Ballad of Anthea Bloodclot* a mocking, bitter piece that unsettled Enrak. When questioned, Ersil admitted he and Anthea were childhood friends, though neither were born in Khaali. His nostalgia was laced with mystery.

Xene Redmayne soon arrived, ensuring their cups stayed full, and conversation gave way to laughter.

## 1PM – Secrets Unfold

The barrel-raft bumped against the dock. Inside, n’Dong was bruised, soaked, and furious. His thief had vanished the moment they landed.

Trapped, n’Dong spent fifteen agonizing minutes trying to break free from within until the barrel finally tipped and he clambered out. Alone among the stolen goods, he cracked open one of the barrels and found a note: “Half the load. Midnight. One Ball Inn” signed with a Broken Fang insignia.

A grin crept across n’Dong’s wet face.

He had his lead.
