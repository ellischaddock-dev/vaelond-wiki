# Session 01: Die Dog, Die

> [!abstract] Session summary
> The party meets during Emberwake and defends Somnolent from a coordinated goblin attack.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 1 |
| **Recorded** | 22 July 2025 at 21:22 |
| **Arc** | Emberwake |

The quiet town of [[Somnolent]] stirred to life with the opening of the [[Emberwake Festival|Emberwake festival]], a celebration of fire, unity, and remembrance. Though joy permeated the air, a sombre undercurrent lingered—the memory of Anthea Bloodclot, the former leader of the Five Clans, whose passing the year prior cast a long shadow over the festivities.

Three voices opened the day:

- **Romayne Drayton**, the village headman, spoke first. His words were carefully chosen—earnest, charismatic, but subdued, honoring Anthea's memory with the dignity it deserved.
- **Alistair Prusset**, the [[The Powers That Be#Perfid|Perfid]] ambassador, followed. He was formal, distant, and faintly dismissive of the festival’s spiritual elements, though he managed to deliver his remarks with a minimal degree of respect.
- Then came **Callum Redmayne**, landlord of the Drowsy Coin, whose raucous charm and infectious energy kicked off the revelry with cheer and drink.

The party, strangers still, spread across the fairgrounds—each drawn into different distractions, unwittingly threading their fates closer together.

## Festival Highlights

- **Erelmor** stepped boldly into competition, losing a drinking contest to the local legend Billy Hill with no loss of dignity. He later tried his hand at archery with the hunter **Terrick**, who noted Erelmor’s latent potential—and shared a troubling rumour of a goblin camp spotted near the forest edge.

- **Enrak** found himself the recipient of a warm gesture from the kind-hearted **Xene**, who offered him festival food with a touch of flirtation. Later, he gambled with the mysterious wizard **Elmwood**, but misread the game—watching the old man’s hands, not the ghostly mage hand that shuffled the cups.

- **Ballar** thundered his way through the games. He bested **Alaaric Hammerfall**, the town treasurer, at dice—perhaps sparking the ember of rivalry. He then crushed the **Hammertoss**, earning the respect of **Balur Moonclaw**, who offered to introduce him to a local fighting pit. Ballar left with two prizes: a **Bag of Rust Tricks** from **Sable**, and a **Heward’s Handy Haversack**.

- **Ieuan** played for subtler stakes. After pinning the tail perfectly on a donkey (to everyone's surprise), he was rewarded with a **magical key**. He won again at a cup shuffle game, but instead of a prize, chose a private follow-up with Elmwood. Before long, **Mother Joygrove** found him and requested his aid for **Father Zantos** during the coming ritual.

## The Goblin Attack

Just as Father Zantos began the Emberwake invocation, celebration turned to chaos. A dog’s chilling death rattle shattered the ceremony, followed by goblin war-songs. Where the crowd fled, four soon-to-be heroes stood firm.

Erelmor and Ballar surged forward, gleefully trying to outdo one another with cries of “watch this!” as they felled goblins in a deadly display of theatre and pride. Meanwhile, Ieuan and Enrak found their fire magic sputtering, failing to ignite fear in the goblins—but **Ieuan’s Entrapment spell** held the line, ensuring the villagers could flee in safety.

Sensing a greater threat, **Ieuan ignited the bonfire**, intending to warn the town—but instead triggered the next wave. The magical phoenix flare, meant to symbolize rebirth, was in fact a **signal for the goblin warchanter** and her kin to join the fray and burn it all.

Still, the party fought on. Enrak, burning with frustration and flame, stepped into the breach to deliver the **final blow**, incinerating the goblin warchanter and halting the assault. As Father Zantos rushed to tend to the wounded, he declared **Enrak the hero of the hour**—a pronouncement that didn’t sit well with his more flamboyant allies.

## Second Wave: Bridge Ambush

No rest for heroes—**a scream rang out** from the bridge. The ambassador, Alistair Prusset, and his loyal hound were under attack. The party sprinted to the scene, but arrived just in time to see the hound’s **throat torn open** by a goblin boss’s dogslicer.

What followed was a mess of half-planned heroics:

- Erelmor finished the goblin dog with efficient violence, before finding the sharp end of a dogslicer and retreating.
- Ballar, ever the opportunist, moved to protect Prusset attempting to slyly curry favour with the ambassador.
- Ieuan and Enrak hurled fire spells again—but the goblin boss seemed warmed at most by the flame, shrugging off their assaults.

A second goblin wave surged from the treeline, surrounding Ballar. From a distance, the others peppered the attackers as best they could.

Eventually, **Erelmor brought down the goblin boss**, and the tide turned. As one last goblin fled and dove into the river to escape, **Ballar drew from his bag of tricks** — and a **giant boar** splashed into being, chasing and crushing the fleeing goblin beneath the water.

## Session End

As the blood settled into the earth and the villagers crept cautiously back to assess the wreckage, the four adventurers found themselves **changed**—strengthened by danger, tempered by fire, and perhaps for the first time, connected by shared purpose.

They **rose to level 2**, not just as survivors, but as the first sparks of something greater.
