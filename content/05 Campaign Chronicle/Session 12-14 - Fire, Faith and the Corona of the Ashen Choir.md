# Sessions 12–14: Fire, Faith and the Corona of the Ashen Choir

> [!abstract] Session summary
> The Defracturers assault the Perfid embassy, rescue Terrick and pursue the Corona as Prusset is murdered in custody.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 12–14 |
| **Recorded** | 26 March 2026 at 19:55 |
| **Arc** | The Perfid embassy |

## Preparation and Poor Decisions

The Defracturers knew what was coming. War, in all but name.

They spent what little time they had preparing — though, as ever, preparation took many forms. Ballar and Erelmor took to the shops, only to find the armour they sought unfinished. Ballar compensated in the only way he knew how: buying an alarming number of healing potions and commissioning a new sign for his bedroom door, priorities firmly intact.

Meanwhile, Ieuan took a more measured approach. Through Noctua, he mapped the [[The Powers That Be#Perfid|Perfid]] Embassy, identifying entrances, weak points, and patterns. It was careful, thoughtful work—the kind that might have changed everything.

It didn’t.

Before departing, Ballar emptied his Bag of Rust Tricks, releasing a petrified goat, a boar, and an owl into their ranks. It was equal parts strategy and spectacle.

Then, with what confidence they could muster, they chose their entry point: The chapel door.

It was, unfortunately, the worst possible choice.

## The Embassy Breach

The battering ram struck once and the embassy answered.

A runic blast detonated outward, throwing the party back in a thunderous shockwave that echoed through the halls. Any hope of stealth died instantly. What followed was chaos.

Waves of Perfidian enforcers poured in — skinheaded zealots and hardened thugs, all rallying around a fanatic of Elgira who radiated fervor and fear in equal measure. The battle was immediate and brutal, with bodies dropping quickly on both sides.

The fanatic proved the greatest threat, twisting the battlefield with fear and divine fury, pushing the Defracturers toward retreat. Then Enrak intervened.

A precise volley of Magic Missiles shattered the fanatic’s concentration, breaking the spell that had begun to unravel the party. The momentum shifted instantly. The Defracturers surged forward, cutting down the remaining enemies and reclaiming control of the room.

For a moment, it seemed they had regained the upper hand. Then everything fell apart.

## Fragmentation

In the aftermath of victory, the party did what they did best: They scattered.

Ballar, driven by instinct — or perhaps something stranger — charged down the main hallway, focused less on the mission and more on claiming territory. His owl followed. His boar… did not, becoming lodged firmly in the doorway.

Ieuan, meanwhile, transformed into a rat and began a solo reconnaissance mission upstairs, quietly inspecting bedrooms with no immediate goal beyond curiosity. This left the rest of the party divided just as danger returned.

Ballar had unknowingly walked into a kill zone: two zealots and six thugs surged toward him. Alone, he took a defensive stance at the doorway, holding them at bay with sheer resilience. Blow after blow glanced off him. It was a remarkable stand. It did not last.

When the others finally forced their way into the hall — thanks to the boar quite literally smashing through the wall — they attempted to assist. Erelmor cast Faerie Fire. It caught Ballar.

Stripped of his defensive advantage, Ballar was overwhelmed in seconds. The tide turned violently. Only Ieuan’s return from his upstairs excursion prevented a complete collapse, allowing the group to rally and finish the remaining enemies. The zealots, however, fled—retreating deeper into the embassy.

## The Descent

The chase led downward. Erelmor vaulted the staircase in pursuit, while Ballar followed—only to be sent crashing down by a hidden rune, the embassy itself fighting back at every turn. Below, in the dungeon, the truth of Perfid’s operation was laid bare.

A torturer, hulking and methodical, stood over a barely conscious Terrick, preparing to finish what had been started. Erelmor hesitated but Ieuan did not. He struck first, disrupting the execution and immediately healing Terrick, pulling him back from the brink. The group descended upon the torturer, overwhelming him with coordinated force.

Among the remains, Ieuan claimed a Periapt of Wound Closure, a small but potent reward.

## Truths and Tunnels

Terrick, battered but alive, shared what he knew. The fanatic and zealots had come through a hidden passage deeper within the dungeon, forcing others out before preparing their own escape. He spoke of interrogation, of refusing to betray the party, and of a strange phrase the fanatic had sought: “The… Coca cola of a shaky wire?”

The party stared. Then Din — speaking through Ballar’s ring — laughed. “Corona of the Ashen Choir.”

The real prize. The real objective.

## The Church of Elgira

The pursuit led through the passage and out into an unexpected location: The [[Perfidian Faith|Church of Elgira]], standing quietly on the edge of [[Somnolent]].

Inside, the final confrontation awaited. The fanatic, now crowned in glowing radiance, stood at the altar alongside the remaining zealots. A portal churned at their feet.

The Defracturers charged.

The fanatic responded instantly—Thunder Step erupted through the room, scattering the party violently. Enrak attempted to answer with overwhelming firepower, unleashing Aganazzar’s Scorcher—only for it to be counterspelled effortlessly.

The power disparity was unmistakable. Before the party could recover, the fanatic and one zealot escaped through the portal, leaving the last behind to ensure their retreat.

They killed him quickly. It changed nothing.

## Ash and Aftermath

With the portal gone and the church searched, the Defracturers stepped outside and found Elmwood Bluevine, Job Ashmere, and a contingent of guards waiting.

The truth came together quickly. The failed embassy assault had triggered a wider response. While the party had pursued the zealots, others had moved to secure Prusset. Elmwood’s reaction was immediate — and alarming. He opened a Dimension Door, dragging Job with him as he shouted: “Back to the Shackles — now!”

When the Defracturers arrived, the smell hit first. Burnt flesh. Burnt hair.

Inside the cell, Alistair Prusset was dead — burned to a charred husk within his cage, with Summer Quaresh and Eden Carrion gone. Enrak protested, against all evidence in front of him, insisting Summer would never do this.

## One Man’s Victory

Not all of the Defracturers shared in the horror. Ballar, seeing opportunity in chaos, had doubled back to the embassy. While the others chased zealots and uncovered conspiracies, he claimed his prize: The master bedroom.

## What Comes Next

By the time Romayne Drayton arrived, the damage was done. The ambassador was dead.

The zealots had escaped. The truth was unraveling faster than anyone could contain it.

Romayne thanked the party, though the weight behind his words was heavier than gratitude. He instructed them to regroup at the embassy in the morning.

The Defracturers agreed and, in true fashion, began planning something utterly absurd in the face of mounting crisis: A grand canapé luncheon.
