# Session 04: Tace Nox

> [!abstract] Session summary
> The party confronts time magic, sacrificial machinery and corrupted relics within Tace Nox.

|---|---|
| **Session** | 4 |
| **Recorded** | 23 September 2025 at 21:20 |

## Relics and Ruin

The puzzle was solved, the path was open — but beyond dark flickering with green light, only deeper shadow waited. A narrow tunnel beckoned, too much of a squeeze for Snippy the bear. As the loyal creature let out a low, uncertain huff, Ballar gave him a gentle pat and a quiet word: “Wait here. We’ll be back.”

They would not.

The next chamber felt colder—not in temperature, but in spirit. At its center rose a column of flickering emerald light, strange and pulsing like a heartbeat. Suspended within were two butterflies, motionless in stasis. To either side, a Chwinga lounged upon stone ottomans, unmoving, unblinking, uncaring.

The party approached gently this time—offering food, soft words, and hesitant hands. The chwinga remained indifferent. At least they weren’t hostile.

The ottomans, however, held more interest. n’Dong stepped up to open one… and failed spectacularly, slipping backward with a thud as if repelled by the stone itself. Ballar, with a grunt and a flex, opened it with ease—finding 100 gold and a Ring of Mind Shielding, which Ballar quietly equipped and hid without mention.

The other ottoman held more gold—shared more freely—and a hag’s eye, which vanished swiftly into Erelmor’s possession.

They turned at last to the green column. An inscription lay etched at its base:

“Slowed by radiance and trapped in time,

Release the static from their binds.

One to protect you from the relics,

One to illuminate the exit.”

They tried everything: brute force, magic, cleverness. But it was n’Dong and Ballar, acting in tandem, who reached into the stream of power and retrieved their rewards:

- A mysterious magical staff, humming with latent time magic.
- A gem of brightness, blazing with inner light.

The moment they were freed, the portcullis ahead slid open, beckoning them onward.

## The crucible of stolen time

They descended into a narrow corridor, each step heavy with tension, until it opened into a long-abandoned laboratory, its very air vibrating with arcane pulses.

There, they found three caged hags, suspended in twisted magical stasis, each one a flickering shell of its former self. In the centre: a dais and plinth, the perfect size for the Orb of Time.

Erelmor, ever impulsive, plunged his hand into one of the magical cages — nearly losing it in the process. Ballar threw himself at a sealed door, to no avail. But Enrak, combing the desk, found:

- A rune-sealed scroll bearing the sigil of a sand timer.
- A note, left by an unknown [[The Sundering of Ardynia|Ardynian]] researcher, explaining the ritual’s purpose: Life, traded for progress.

So, with no other option presenting itself, they began. Erelmor, first to act, placed the orb and initiated the siphoning ritual. As arcane light wrapped around him, one hag crumbled to ash—and he felt restored, empowered, like after a long rest. A gift… bought with a life. n’Dong followed, then Ieuan, who watched as the second hag crumbled to dust. The sealed door opened, allowing further passage—but the final hag remained, twitching in half-life.

They hesitated. Morality battled pragmatism. Then Ballar, in an act of grim compromise, tossed a goat from his Bag of Rust Tricks onto the dais. The goat bleated once, then froze in mid-scream as the ritual drained it. The third hag died. The goat turned to stone.

Before they could reflect, a chilling sound cut through the ruins. Snippy—their stalwart bear—was dying.

## The guardian and the relics

Racing through the newly opened path, the party arrived at a grand stone chamber where the Guardian awaited—its massive form carved in Ardynian fashion, animated, and now barring their exit.

In silence, it extended a hand, beckoning the party to return the staff. n’Dong refused. Erelmor fired.

The guardian reeled back — but not in retreat. It stepped aside, revealing the true threat: relics of hags and dwarves, corrupted by the same magic that had defiled these ruins, now rising to attack. Ieuan, with sudden clarity, darted forward to yank the staff from n’Dong’s grasp and place it in the guardian’s hand.

The construct nodded — and responded with power. A protective aura washed over the party, granting bonus vitality and resistance for the battle ahead. Then the room erupted into war.

## The time war

The Relics were monsters of another age — screeching, relentless, forged from bone and corruption. The party met them head-on.

- Ieuan, now a hulking elk, gored and slammed his way through the battlefield.
- Ballar, as ever, stood unflinching in the storm, holding the line against four dwarven relics — all weakened by Snippy’s final stand.
- Erelmor and Enrak flung spells like fire and daggers, dancing between foes.
- n’Dong, cold and calculating, let loose his arcane fury.

The guardian fought beside them… but was the first to fall. Its stone frame cracked and shattered under the hags’ savage assault, the time-magic staff falling to the floor. The tide turned.

Ieuan, desperate to shift momentum, cast Faerie Fire, lighting the enemies like candles — despite catching Ballar in the blaze. It wasn’t enough. Ballar went down, blood soaking the ruin floor. Ieuan followed, cut down by a hag’s screeching magic.

Now just three remained. Outnumbered, outmatched, and nearly out of spells, Enrak, Erelmor, and n’Dong made their final stand. There was no glory — only grit. They fought with the cold knowledge that failure here meant dying forgotten in an ancient tomb.

And then, through the smoke and blood, n’Dong raised his hand. A Magic Missile, three bolts of pure force. The chamber fell silent.

## The Price of Power

The remaining few still standing dragged their allies back from the brink with potions and trembling hands. Together, they searched the rubble — reclaiming the Staff of Broken Time, and prying free emeralds from the guardian’s shattered face.

Victory. At last.

But as they stepped across the threshold to leave… everything changed.

The staff vanished. The gems disintegrated. The Ardynian ruin, ever greedy, took back its gifts. What it left them… was horror.

The quiet clearing of Thistlewound was gone. In its place, a slaughterfield. Blood soaked the earth, limbs and steel lay scattered, the grass torn to shreds.

And among the carnage, the party stood blinking—alive, but only just. And the war, it seemed, had already begun.
