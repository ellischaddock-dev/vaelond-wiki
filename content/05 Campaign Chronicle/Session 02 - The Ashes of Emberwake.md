# Session 02: The Ashes of Emberwake

> [!abstract] Session summary
> The first clues point towards Buggie and a Crow as the party follows Terrick towards Thistlewound.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 2 |
| **Recorded** | 12 August 2025 at 22:12 |
| **Arc** | Emberwake and Thistlewound |

The firelight had faded, but the embers still glowed. In the aftermath of the goblin attack, *[[Somnolent]]* stirred with caution. The party, newly forged in battle, now stood beneath smoke-choked skies unsure of what came next.

**Ambassador Prusset**, still reeling from the death of his faithful hound, met the group in grim silence. **Ballar** stepped forward with a gesture of respect, offering to see to the dog’s burial personally. Prusset, ever the diplomat, declined—keeping his grief neatly folded behind icy eyes.

Before tensions could rise further, **Romayne Drayton** arrived to reclaim control. Measured and authoritative, the village headman called for calm, asked the party to visit him in the morning, and promised to speak of next steps.

The group searched the goblin dead and found two strange clues:

- A **note** scrawled in barely-legible goblin:
  *"No kill, just scare. Get Pruzet. Crow want alive. Buggie."*
- A **large silver coin**, odd in make and unmarked by any known mint.

Prusset denied knowledge of the name "Buggie," and Drayton, while unfamiliar with the goblin, noted with unease that "Crow" had once referred to **Anthea Bloodclot’s hidden agents**, the quiet, loyal few who operated in her shadow.

The heroes took their leave, agreeing to regroup at the Drowsy Coin in the morning. Each went their own way—with Ballar returning to the **[[The Powers That Be#Doro|Doro]] consulate**. There, he encountered **Ambassador Ouin**, a woman of silk and sharp instincts, caught off guard by the muddy menagerie that trailed behind Ballar. Upon hearing of Ballar’s involvement in the ambassador’s rescue, Ouin gave an approving—if weary—nod, but warned: *“Remember where your loyalties lie. Doro first. Always.”*

## Picking up Threads and Receipts

Morning broke with grease and gossip. Over **breakfast boar baps** at the Drowsy Coin, the group reunited and learned that **Elmwood**, the local wizard, had performed powerful magic to protect the tavern and its patrons during the attack—a fact shared with no small amount of admiration by **Callum Redmayne**, which seemed to inspire some jealousy in **Enrak**.

They then returned to **Romayne Drayton**, who spoke openly and plainly. With the village still rattled and official aid slow to arrive, he asked for the party’s support in uncovering the truth behind the attack. To assist, he offered one of his trusted agents—**n’Dong**.

Drayton laid bare the fragile unity left in Anthea’s wake. She had been more than a leader—she was the very thread holding the Five Clans together. Her “Crows” were real, though anonymous. Now, only the **Voruun** and **Khaali** still marched toward her dream. **Erzhin** had withdrawn, and **Orvak**, plagued by its **[[Rootbound]]** movement, teetered on the edge of internal collapse. Surprisingly, he also admitted that Bloodclot had very few loyal to her in Somnolent, a town that prided itself on its neutrality and ability to stay out of clan skirmishes, but if anyone was still loyal to Anthea then **Eden**, the Voruun liaison, would know.

With no clear trail to follow but the goblin note, Drayton suggested seeking out the hunter **Terrick** or reviewing the **report** he had submitted.

Thus, the party sought out **Alaaric Hammerfall**, treasurer of Somnolent and a man not easily parted from his ledgers. After some bartering, Alaaric sold them **Terrick’s report** for a single gold coin. Ballar, ever the dealmaker, pressed further—inquiring about a report on **Anthea’s death**. Alaaric confirmed its existence, but explained the delicate material would take some time to procure.

## Chasing Waterfalls

With the trail confirmed, the party visited Terrick himself in the **village arcade**. He was eager to help—though **Erelmor**, in an almost performative show of suspicion, accused the man of failing to hunt his own rabbits. Only **Ieuan** and **n’Dong’s** timely intervention calmed the rising tension. Terrick offered to guide the group to **Thistlewound**, the site of his goblin sighting, but warned they should arrive before nightfall.

Their journey was swift but not uneventful. Hours into the trip, they arrived at a river, where a **waterfall roared across their path**. Terrick, showing surprising agility, demonstrated a hidden **rope swing** and leapt across with ease.

**Ieuan**, inspired by the display, attempted the same—and promptly botched it. With a *sickening splash*, he slammed into the river and immediately drew the attention of a **crocodile**. As the beast snapped its jaws around him, **Ballar dove in** to assist, slashing at thin air as the rest of the party fired spells and arrows.

With death looming, **Ieuan** thrust his hands into the beast’s mouth and unleashed a toxic blast of **poison spray** down its throat, felling it from within. Terrick pulled him to safety as the other crocs were dispatched.

Amid the aftermath, **Ieuan spotted something behind the waterfall**. Working with **Enrak**, the two used their **mage hands** to retrieve a waterlogged **wooden cache**, which contained **eight healing potions**—a windfall they would soon be grateful for.

## Obvious Riddles & Unexpected Bullywugs

The final leg of their journey led them to the crumbling edges of **Thistlewound Ruins**, overgrown and silent.

**Ieuan**, attuned to whispers on the wind, caught goblin voices murmuring in the distance. Soon after, the group encountered a **sealed stone door** bearing an inscription:

*The moon and stars may show the way, To knowledge lost, kept sealed away.*

*I guard what sleeps where silence grows, No flame may flicker, no lantern glow.*

*In still of dark, speak sleep's last shout, Snuff the candles and call lights out.*

Ieuan instantly divined the meaning: *Return at night, in darkness, and speak “Lights Out.”* It was, in his own words, *“obvious.”*

With time to kill, the party scouted further—**Erelmor**, perhaps too eager, **triggered a trap**, alerting a mix of **goblin and bullywug guards**. The ensuing battle was dramatic, chaotic, and deeply pyrotechnic.

- Spells flew with reckless abandon.
- **Erelmor** doused a bullywug in oil just to see it burn.
- **n’Dong** and **Enrak** emptied their arcane reserves in wild displays of poison and flame.

More enemies surged from the shadows—but just before bloodshed could resume, a massive **bugbear** emerged from the cave entrance, barking a command:

> **“Enough! If you want answers, we talk. Not fight.”**

The battlefield fell still.
