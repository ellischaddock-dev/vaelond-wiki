# Session 09: The Capture of Prusset

> [!abstract] Session summary
> After a desperate running battle, the party captures Prusset but fractures over who should receive him.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 9 |
| **Recorded** | 23 December 2025 at 22:18 |
| **Arc** | The Prusset affair |

The hunt for Ambassador Alistair Prusset resumed in a blur of sweat, arcane flashes, and increasingly unhinged improvisation. The party — bruised, burned, and borderline delirious—picked up their pursuit with desperate energy, knowing every second counted.

A trap was set: ball bearings rolled across the alley, grease spells slicked the flagstones, and even a shattered vat of vegetable stock was spread to turn the terrain into a soupy mess. It was crude, it was chaotic, and it was almost beautiful.

But Prusset blinked through it, vanishing from their perfect trap with maddening grace. Again and again, he appeared where he shouldn’t be, darting between houses, alleyways, and outcroppings like smoke through fingers. The Defracturers grew more desperate, hounding him at every turn, bounding across the rooftops as they tried to keep sight of their quarry.

Amid the chase, Ballar felt a foreign presence try to touch his mind — a whisper of telepathic contact — but he rejected it instantly, using his ring of mind shielding as he refused to allow anything to break his focus. Then, the tide turned.

Just as Prusset’s arcane reserve ran dry, his reinforcements arrived — two robed priests of Elgira, casting radiant fire and divine protections. The battle became a blur: Erelmor, Ieuan, n’Dong, and Enrak all fell at different points, strewn across [[Somnolent]]’s quiet backstreets like broken chess pieces.

But slowly, the party rallied. They concentrated fire, burned spells, swung wildly. Ballar, bloodied and raging, knocked Prusset unconscious — only for the priests to bring him back again and again.

At one absurd point in the melee, as fire and death rained around them, n’Dong paused mid-combat to offer to repair Ballar’s manacles. “Now is not the time,” someone probably screamed.

Eventually, the priests fell, and Prusset, gasping, broken and bloodied, was finally subdued and shackled — hogtied like a prize boar.

Victory brought no peace. Ballar, always thinking five moves ahead (and ten degrees to the left), suggested they take Prusset to the [[The Powers That Be#Doro|Doro]] embassy first, arguing it was only right that Doro, not Somnolent, handle the matter.

But the rest of the party — led by Ieuan’s sense of duty and n’Dong’s keen paranoia — refused. Their loyalty was with Romayne Drayton and Somnolent’s fragile peace. The split was final. Furious and humiliated, Ballar stormed away from the group, his face a silent scream.

Back at the embassy, Stacii was waiting—livid. She revealed the truth: the voice in Ballar’s mind had been Ambassador Ouin, urgently telling him to stay with the party and deliver Prusset to Drayton.

“You should not have left them,” she snapped. “Didn't you get Ouin's message?!” Ballar didn’t respond. He just turned and walked away.

Back with the rest of the group, a new plan formed. Ieuan would locate Romayne, while the others would drag the unconscious ambassador to The Shackles, the makeshift Somnolent jail. Ieuan, tired and hopeful, made his way toward Drayton’s estate—only to run straight into Ballar, seething and silent. Before words could be properly exchanged, Ballar did what he had clearly been building toward for hours: He punched Ieuan in the face.

The paladin marched off toward the river, trying to find a piece of peace in a world that no longer made sense.

Meanwhile, Erelmor, Enrak, and n’Dong arrived at The Shackles. Deputy Gayle Bramble, still yawning through a dawn shift, was not prepared to see three bloodied adventurers and a gagged, chained ambassador. Her jaw dropped. Her eyes widened. But to her credit, she quickly stepped into duty.

She unlocked a cell, allowed them to store the prisoner inside, and immediately sent a runner to fetch Drayton. The Defracturers, now scattered and wounded, had done it: Prusset was in chains.

But victory had come at a steep cost—fractured friendships, bruised egos, and the looming threat of greater powers moving in the shadows. Somnolent was no longer a sleepy town.

And the Defracturers? They were no longer just adventurers. They were now players in a dangerous game.
