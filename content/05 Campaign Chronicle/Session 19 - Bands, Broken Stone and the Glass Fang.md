# Session 19: Bands, Broken Stone and the Glass Fang

> [!abstract] Session summary
> The golem falls, the relic is secured and Broken Fang scouts confront the party as they leave Rana Viam.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Session** | 19 |
| **Recorded** | 23 June 2026 at 13:23 |
| **Arc** | Rana Viam |

Battered and bruised from their previous defeat, the Defracturers finally took the long rest they had so thoroughly earned. For once, the night passed with only minimal disturbance from Erelmor, whose nightmares remained troubling by ordinary standards, but were positively tranquil by his own. It was as close to peace as the ruins of Rana Viam seemed willing to offer.

During the rest, Ieuan devoted time to the Star Seed gifted to him by Father Zantos. Under his careful study, a single leaf unfurled, and within it he recognized the constellation of his Starry Form. It was a quiet but significant moment—one of growth and affirmation amidst the oppressive stone and strange time magic of the ruin. Elsewhere, the rest of the party focused on a more immediate concern: how, exactly, they were going to kill the golem that had crushed them so thoroughly the last time.

By the end of their rest, the group felt renewed. More than renewed, in fact — there was a fresh sharpness to them, a greater confidence and power humming beneath the surface. They prepared themselves thoroughly. Mage Armour was cast, Aid was bestowed, a steed was summoned, and the group moved back toward the golem chamber with far more caution and coordination than before.

When they entered, they found the golem once again inactive, looming in silence as though it had never annihilated them in the first place. For a moment, the chamber held still. Then Ieuan, accepting that there would be no progress without risk, stepped forward and investigated the dais, reactivating the construct. What followed was, by Defracturer standards, a masterpiece of planning.

Ieuan and Ballar took immediate control of the battlefield, repeatedly using Command to halt the golem’s movements and rob it of momentum. While the construct stood helpless under their magic, the rest of the group unloaded everything they had. Fire, force, steel, and spellwork hammered into the stone giant. Erelmor quickly discovered that poison was useless against a construct of solid stone, but by then the party had plenty of other ways to hurt it. n’Dong further locked the golem down with a well-placed Web, making the great brute even easier to punish.

For a time, it was almost surgical. The golem was stalled, restrained, and systematically dismantled as the party poured their strongest magic into it. Eventually, though, as their most powerful spells began to run dry, the construct broke through their control and fought back. The chamber filled with the sound of grinding stone and thunderous impacts as the golem began swinging its colossal fists, slowing the party and forcing them onto the defensive. But by then, the Defracturers could sense it: this was not the rise of a second defeat, but the death rattle of a great enemy.

They pressed the advantage. At last, the golem collapsed, crashing to its knees before exploding into a heap of broken rubble. Victory was theirs.

The group rushed to the remains and quickly recovered the Bands of Distortion, the ornate relics the construct had been wearing. Ieuan, once again proving invaluable in matters arcane, investigated the wreckage and determined that the bracers were linked to the golem through a heart stone. If they took that stone with them as well, the bracers would remain bound to the party rather than vanishing back into the ruin the moment they tried to leave.

That led, inevitably, to a discussion about whether any of them should attempt to wear or use the bracers immediately. Thankfully, good sense prevailed.

When Erelmor found a fitted holding case for the relic among the remains, the group chose caution over greed and stored the bracers away safely. They also gathered whatever other riches they could, including a sealed [[The Sundering of Ardynia|Ardynian]] scroll, before turning their attention to the next question.

Should they press their luck and go back through the ruin to loot everything they had missed, or take their victory and leave? The debate split the group neatly in two. Some saw the chance for more treasure. Others felt they had already pushed fate far enough. In the end, compromise won out: they would take a short rest, then leave Rana Viam and make for Hushvale, where they could recover properly and decide their next move from safety.

The world, however, was not done with them yet. As they left the ruin, they found a stranger waiting for them on the stairs outside. He ordered them all to stay exactly where they were.

That, unsurprisingly, was not well received.

As two other scouts made a break for it deep into the woods; n’Dong, already on edge and immediately recognizing the Broken Fang sigil on the scout’s lapel, wasted no time at all. He surged forward, grappled the man, and hurled him back down into the entrance of Rana Viam before leaping after him and demanding answers. The scout, terrified and completely outmatched, blurted out the most important name he could have chosen:

Ulsar Quinn.

The name hit n’Dong like a physical blow. Ulsar had once commanded the unit n’Dong had served in before his disastrous expedition into the Ardynian ruins — before his transformation, before his escape from the Broken Fang. This was not just another scout. This was a thread tying directly back into the darkest parts of n’Dong’s past.

As the rest of the group caught up and took in the scene, the scout confirmed something even worse: their horses had been taken. Ulsar had them. That was enough to turn the entire moment from tense encounter into looming crisis.

Ballar began to speak, no doubt ready to pry more useful information from the man. But before he could get far, Erelmor drew back his dagger, clearly ready to end the conversation—and the scout — then and there.

And there the session hung: with the Bands finally won, the ruin behind them, the Broken Fang suddenly in front of them, and the next move poised on the edge of a knife.
