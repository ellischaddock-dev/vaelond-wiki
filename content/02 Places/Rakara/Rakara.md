# Rakara

Rakara is a southern realm shaped by five great clans, each with its own territory, customs and ambitions. For decades those clans moved towards a shared confederation under **Anthea Bloodclot**, the Lord of the Five Clans. Her assassination left the title empty, the union uncertain and every clan watching the others closely.
