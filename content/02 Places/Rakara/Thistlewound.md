# Thistlewound

> [!summary] At a glance
> A ruined forest site several hours from Somnolent that became a refuge for Buggie's displaced goblins, bugbears and bullywugs before a massacre destroyed the camp.

## Approach

The route from [[02 Places/Rakara/Somnolent/Somnolent|Somnolent]] to Thistlewound crosses a river and waterfall using a concealed rope swing. [[03 People/Notable Figures/Terrick Lowgins|Terrick Lowgins]] guided the Defracturers there after reporting goblin activity in the area.

The outer ruins were overgrown, quiet and protected by traps and sentries.

## Buggie's refuge

Thistlewound was occupied by [[03 People/Notable Deceased/Buggie|Buggie]] and a mixed group of goblins, bugbears and bullywugs displaced from Minir.

Although members of the camp had taken part in the Emberwake attack, Buggie described his people as refugees who had entered a mercenary arrangement rather than a force seeking open war with Somnolent.

Buggie negotiated with the Defracturers and exchanged information concerning the Loyal Crow who had hired his group, the attempted abduction of Alistair Prusset and a House Bromere favour coin.

## The Ardynian threshold

A sealed stone door within the ruins bore a riddle concerning darkness, stars and extinguished light. Speaking **“Lights Out”** at night opened the threshold.

The chambers beyond led to the Ardynian site known as [[02 Places/Rakara/Tace Nox|Tace Nox]].

## The massacre

When the party returned from Tace Nox, the clearing had become a slaughterfield.

Known details include:

- Buggie and most of the camp's inhabitants had been killed.
- The victims showed wounds caused by both blades and claws.
- An unidentified drow corpse was found among the dead.
- Survivors said the attackers had been searching for a note stolen by Buggie's group.
- Buggie had torn an insignia from one attacker that Erelmor recognised as belonging to the [[04 Organisations/Rootbound|Rootbound]].
- Terrick and one terrified goblin survived the immediate attack.

The party searched the caves, recovered the strange silver coin from beneath Buggie's stone throne and then set fire to the camp.

## Current state

Thistlewound was left burned, blood-soaked and apparently abandoned. Its later condition is unknown.

The threshold leading to Tace Nox may remain accessible to anyone who knows how to open it.
