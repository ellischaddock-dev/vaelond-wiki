# Tace Nox

> [!summary] At a glance
> An Ardynian ruin accessed through [[02 Places/Rakara/Thistlewound|Thistlewound]], containing temporal machinery, sacrificial research, preserved creatures and the [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Staff of Broken Time|Staff of Broken Time]].

## Entrance and light puzzle

A sealed stone threshold at Thistlewound opened only after nightfall when the command **“Lights Out”** was spoken in darkness.

Beyond the entrance, the party found chambers illuminated by sickly green light, dead robed figures and an Ardynian soldier statue. A mosaic indicated that the ruin's first mechanism required an **arrow of light** directed towards an altar. Solving the puzzle opened a portal into the deeper site identified as Tace Nox.

## Chambers of stasis

Within Tace Nox, the Defracturers encountered:

- butterflies suspended within a column of emerald temporal energy;
- two indifferent chwinga resting on stone ottomans;
- a [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Ring of Mind Shielding|Ring of Mind Shielding]];
- a Gem of Brightness;
- the Staff of Broken Time.

The ring later revealed that it contained a soul calling itself [[03 People/Notable Figures/Din|Din]].

## The laboratory

A deeper laboratory contained three hags held within magical cages, a dais sized for the **Orb of Time**, and a note describing the site's principle:

**Life, traded for progress.**

Using the Orb activated a siphoning process that restored members of the party while destroying the imprisoned hags. A creature summoned from Ballar's Bag of Rust Tricks was also petrified during the process.

The party removed a rune-sealed Ardynian scroll from the laboratory. The scroll later became an important reason for their meeting with [[03 People/Notable Figures/Elmwood Bluevine|Elmwood Bluevine]].

## Guardian and corrupted relics

A stone guardian initially demanded the return of the Staff of Broken Time. When Ieuan placed the staff into its hand, the construct aided the party against corrupted hag and dwarven relics.

The guardian was destroyed during the battle, but the Defracturers defeated the remaining creatures and recovered the staff from the rubble.

## The ruin's claim

When the party crossed the threshold to leave:

- the Staff of Broken Time vanished and returned to the ruin;
- emeralds removed from the guardian disintegrated;
- the Ring of Mind Shielding and sealed scroll remained with the party.

Tace Nox therefore appears able to reclaim at least some objects removed from its interior.

The party emerged into the aftermath of the [[02 Places/Rakara/Thistlewound|Thistlewound massacre]]. It is unclear how much time passed outside while they explored the ruin.

## Current state

- The entrance puzzle has been solved.
- The principal guardian was destroyed.
- The Staff of Broken Time remains somewhere within the site.
- The laboratory's imprisoned hags were destroyed.
- The site may still contain active temporal mechanisms and unexplored chambers.
