# Rakara: A Land Once Wild, Now Watchful

For much of its history, Rakara was a patchwork of clashing steppe tribes: nomadic, fiercely independent and in near-constant flux. Each held to its own gods, laws and grudges. Blood feuds outlasted generations. Trade was a gamble. War was tradition.

Over the past three centuries, five dominant powers gradually absorbed, allied with or displaced their smaller rivals:

- [[Clan Orvak]]
- [[Clan Erzhin]]
- [[Clan Temir]]
- [[Clan Khaali]]
- [[Clan Voruun]]

## The Five Clans

The resulting clans were never a single state, but their influence imposed a recognisable political shape on the region. Each retained its own leaders, customs, military and laws.

The supposed office of the **Lord of the Five Clans** was an old story rather than an established institution. Ten years ago, **Anthea Bloodclot** adopted the title and used the myth to create a personal following. Through diplomacy, leverage and force of personality, she acted as war-broker and adjudicator, encouraging shared laws, reducing raids and pooling knowledge between the clans.

Anthea rose to prominence in [[Clan Khaali|Khaali lands]], and many outsiders therefore assume she was Khaali by origin. That assumption is incorrect: her true place of origin was never publicly established.

## A crown-shaped vacuum

One year ago, Anthea was murdered near [[Somnolent]]. No formal office died with her, but the network of agreements and personal loyalties she had built began to unravel.

Voruun publicly claims the right to succeed her and continue the project of unity. Only Khaali openly supports that claim. Erzhin, Orvak and Temir have not accepted Voruun's position.

- Some clans have retreated inward.
- Others compete for influence over the central roads.
- Somnolent's independence has become more strategically important.
- Foreign churches, traders and diplomats are taking a greater interest in the region.

## Geography

Rakara lies between Doro to the west and Minir to the east. The inaccessible **Sera Wilds** form the great mountain barrier to the north and prevent any direct route between Rakara and Perfid.

Orvak and Erzhin do not occupy the sealed interior of the range. Their communities lie in the forests, foothills, mountain bases and accessible outer valleys of the Sera Wilds.

## The roads that hold Rakara together

Two routes form the bones of Rakara's trade network:

- The **Salt Spine** runs north to south through the heart of the region.
- The **Trade Horizon** crosses east to west between Doro and Minir.

Their intersection near Somnolent gives central Rakara enormous strategic importance.
