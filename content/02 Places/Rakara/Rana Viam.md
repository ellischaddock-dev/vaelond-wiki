> [!summary] At a glance

## Approach and entrance

The Defracturers reached Rana Viam while travelling through Orvak towards Hushvale. The ruin lies towards the outskirts of Orvak, close to the border of [[02 Places/Rakara/Clans/Clan Voruun|Voruun]] territory. It is about two to three days' ride from [[02 Places/Rakara/Somnolent/Somnolent|Somnolent]] and about half a day's ride from [[02 Places/Rakara/Hushvale|Hushvale]].

Evidence within and around the site showed that members of the [[04 Organisations/Broken Fang|Broken Fang]] had previously searched the ruin and died there.

The entrance presented a sequence built around the commands **jump**, **cross** and **run**, establishing the ruin's pattern of testing movement, sacrifice and understanding rather than relying on an ordinary locked door.

## Vaelen's warning

Within the ruin, the party encountered a recorded message or preserved communication from **Saint Vaelen**.

The message identified relics used in the Sundering, indicated that Vaelen had hidden some of them and warned of conflict among surviving ancient figures. Vaelen expressed particular concern about Marra pursuing war at any cost and Desmon seeking divine power, while presenting Erith as a possible ally.

The message expanded the party's understanding of the ancient saints, but it did not prove that [[03 People/Notable Deceased/Anthea Bloodclot|Anthea Bloodclot]] was Saint Marra.

## The crossing chamber

One chamber forced the party to cross lanes of moving creatures and hazards. Collisions returned travellers to the start and repeated failure risked exhaustion.

A central dais accepted sacrifices of personal power, such as spellcasting resources, in return for further opportunities. The warning associated with the trial suggested that giving nothing would lead to failure.

The party eventually crossed after several dangerous attempts.

## The golem and the Bands

A great stone golem guarded the [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Bands of Distortion|Bands of Distortion]]. The party's first attempt ended in a decisive defeat and retreat.

After resting and preparing, the Defracturers returned. They controlled the construct with command magic and webbing before destroying it. Ieuan determined that the Bands were linked to the golem through a heart stone. The party recovered both and stored the relic in a fitted case.

A sealed Ardynian scroll was also recovered.

## Current state

- The guardian golem has been destroyed.
- The Bands of Distortion and their heart stone have been removed.
- The party left the ruin at the end of Session 19.
- Broken Fang scouts were waiting outside, beginning the pursuit recorded in Session 20.
- The pursuit culminated in the destruction of Ulsar Quinn's nearby Broken Fang camp in Session 21.
- It is not clear whether every chamber or function of Rana Viam has been discovered.
