# Rakara's Regions and Clans

There are five great clans in Rakara—five names spoken like compass points, shaping law, war and the reception offered to a stranger at the fire. Once there were dozens. Time, trade and blood reduced them to five.

The title **Lord of the Five Clans** was never an official office. It was an old myth revived by Anthea Bloodclot, who used it for ten years to build a personal following and hold a fragile web of agreements together. Her death left no constitutional vacancy, but it did leave a dangerous absence of influence.

## Clan Orvak

Known as the **Quiet Root of Rakara**, [[Clan Orvak]] occupies northeastern forests and the lower foothills of the Sera Wilds. Orvaki communities do not extend into the inaccessible heart of the range. They are hardy farmers, hunters and foragers who value action over words and are regarded as stubborn but dependable.

Orvak is the most diverse of the clans, home to many ancestries and customs united by respect for the land. Its long border with Doro makes it wary of political and economic encroachment.

## Clan Erzhin

[[Clan Erzhin]] lives in the accessible mountain bases, outer valleys and cliff settlements of the northeastern Sera Wilds. The **Erzhini** are a people of record and reflection who revere wind and stone. Their histories are cut into cliff faces and mountain halls.

Led by the siblings **Malen** and **Talia**, the clan balances diplomacy with martial strength. Its close ties to Minir cause concern among other clans, but Erzhin remains fiercely protective of its own autonomy and Rakara's memory.

## Clan Temir

[[Clan Temir]] rules the storm-wracked southern coast. The Temiri value strength, discipline and action over words. Their warriors, fishers and sailors worship the [[Rakaran Faiths#Sea-Mother and Moon-Father|Sea-Mother and Moon-Father]].

Temir's ports have long served merchants bypassing Doro's inland controls. The construction of a rival Voruun harbour has sharply increased tension. Under **Avaz** and the Wavecutters, Temir remains proud and prepared for conflict.

## Clan Khaali

[[Clan Khaali]] occupies fertile southeastern Rakara beneath dormant **Mount Khal**. Its capital, **Emberrest**, is known for art, agriculture, fire worship and trained pyromancers.

Anthea Bloodclot rose to prominence in Khaali lands, causing the widespread but incorrect assumption that she originated there. **Chieftain Sifaya Flameborn** was one of her closest allies and remains the strongest supporter of Voruun's claim to continue Anthea's project.

## Clan Voruun

[[Clan Voruun]] dominates central Rakara and controls the **Salt Spine** and **Trade Horizon**. It combines expanding cities and diplomatic skill with fighting pits, conquest-driven politics and advancement through bloodshed.

**Relka of the Chain-Eye** publicly claims that Voruun is Anthea's rightful successor and should lead a renewed Rakaran union. Only Khaali openly supports this position. Orvak, Erzhin and Temir have not accepted the claim.

The **Vorunni** present themselves as the future of Rakara. Their neighbours remain unsure whether that future means cooperation or conquest.

## Somnolent

[[Somnolent]] is an independent town where the Salt Spine and Trade Horizon meet. Although surrounded by Voruun's territorial claims, it has never submitted to clan rule.

Its open markets, foreign missions and spiritual pluralism make it a centre of trade and diplomacy. Foreign support for its independence is widely known to occur, though the exact arrangements remain a poorly kept secret rather than an openly acknowledged policy.

The phoenix is Somnolent's **civic spiritual symbol**, representing renewal, resilience and independence. It is celebrated most visibly during the [[Emberwake Festival]].
