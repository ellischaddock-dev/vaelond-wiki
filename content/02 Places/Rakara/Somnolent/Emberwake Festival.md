# The Emberwake Festival

The **Emberwake Festival** is [[Somnolent|Somnolent's]] most beloved celebration: an annual rite held at the dawn of spring to mark rebirth, forgiveness and the chance to begin again.

## Origins

Emberwake began as a small ritual observed by early settlers and traders. They gathered to burn old ledgers, worn goods and scraps of parchment representing burdens or misfortunes from the year before. What began as an act of letting go gradually became a beacon of second chances known across Vaelond.

## Opening the festival

The celebration takes place in **Festival Square** and is traditionally opened by three speakers:

1. The Headman of Somnolent, offering words of hope and reflection.
2. The town's leading innkeeper, blessing the crowd and providing free food and ale.
3. A rotating guest—often a cleric, merchant, performer or other visitor—offering a tale or blessing connected to renewal.

After the speeches, the streets fill with music, fire-dancers, games, communal feasts and storytelling. Stalls sell charms, masks and trinkets representing the shedding of the past. Festivalgoers cast old tokens, letters and keepsakes into ceremonial braziers as acts of symbolic cleansing.

## The great fire

As evening falls, Somnolent's **Elder Druid** approaches a towering structure of timber and herbs in the centre of the square. The pyre is constructed over several days and crowned with the phoenix, Somnolent's civic spiritual symbol.

A single ember taken from the hearth of the town's oldest home ignites the fire. Local tradition holds that a regret whispered into the flames will be carried away in the smoke, leaving space for a new beginning.

## The fire that faltered

Last year's festival was intended to be the grandest in living memory. **Anthea Bloodclot** had agreed to appear as the honoured guest speaker, an unprecedented gesture of cooperation between independent Somnolent and the movement she had built around the Five Clans.

The words never came.

On the morning of Emberwake, Anthea was found dead **inside her caravan**, stationed approximately two miles east of Somnolent along the southern road. At the time, the public knew little more than that she had died from unknown means. The crime scene and caravan disappeared during the festival, preventing a full public investigation.

The town erupted in confusion. Some demanded that the festival be cancelled. Others argued that a rite of renewal should honour Anthea with light rather than silence. Headman [[Romayne Drayton]] chose to continue. A local bard filled Anthea's place with a mournful song, and the Elder Druid lit the bonfire beneath a cloud of fear and suspicion.

That year's fire did not cleanse so much as mark a fracture—a scar written in ash and ember.

## What the party later learned

The Defracturers later procured [[Report on the Death of Anthea Bloodclot|Alaaric Hammerfall's official report]]. It records evidence that Anthea was poisoned with **Bloodspore**, probably administered in wine, and that the original scene was removed by unknown parties before it could be properly examined.
