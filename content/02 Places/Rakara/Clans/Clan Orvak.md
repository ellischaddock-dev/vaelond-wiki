## Sigil

![[Clan Orvak Sigil.png]]

The sigil of Clan Orvak bears a **sturdy oak tree on a forest-green shield**, with a mountain silhouette at its base. The roots extend into the mountain, representing grounded strength, endurance and quiet resolve.

- **Oak tree:** resilience, ancestral wisdom and deep-rooted tradition.
- **Mountain base:** the Sera Wild foothills and the clan's enduring bond with a difficult land.

## Background

Clan Orvak is known as the **Quiet Root of Rakara**. Its cold, damp homeland lies in forested cradles beneath the Sera Wild mountains, where farming is difficult and endurance essential. The Orvaki are hardy, soft-spoken and deeply tied to the land, with a reverence for wilderness that borders on the spiritual.

Their society is clannish but egalitarian. Small, semi-autonomous villages are led by councils of elders. Although **Balka** serves as the clan's figurehead, he gives those councils considerable freedom and responsibility. This decentralised governance has made Orvak particularly resistant to outside control. Anthea Bloodclot's success in bringing them into the confederation was regarded as a diplomatic masterstroke, even in Doro.

## People and culture

Orvaki people tend to be **stoic, slow to trust and slower to forgive**. They prize self-sufficiency, resilience and communal loyalty. Outsiders sometimes mistake their quietness for backwardness—a dangerous assumption.

The Orvaki are especially skilled in:

- Woodland survival and guerrilla tactics.
- Hunting and trapping, including knowledge of rare creatures from the Sera Wilds.
- Herbal alchemy and wilderness medicine.
- Spiritual traditions venerating forest spirits, ancestor shades and weather gods.

Despite its isolation, Orvak is the most racially and culturally diverse of the five clans. It has a long history of accepting exiles and people fleeing persecution elsewhere. In Orvaki lands, quiet contribution matters more than bloodline.

## Known campaign site

[[02 Places/Rakara/Rana Viam|Rana Viam]], an Ardynian Chronarch ruin explored by the Defracturers, lies in Orvak near the outskirts where Orvak borders [[02 Places/Rakara/Clans/Clan Voruun|Voruun]] territory. It is about half a day by horse from [[02 Places/Rakara/Hushvale|Hushvale]] and about two to three days by horse from [[02 Places/Rakara/Somnolent/Somnolent|Somnolent]]. The party recovered the Bands of Distortion there after defeating a stone golem.
## Trade and tensions

Orvak shares a long and tense border with Doro:

- Doro values Orvaki pelts, rare herbs and mountain ore, and border settlements profit from seasonal trade.
- Orvak resents Doroan political meddling and attempts to gain influence through diplomacy or coin.
- Many Orvaki fear that Doro would gladly absorb them—and that cultural and economic annexation has already begun.

Within Rakara, the other clans tend to regard Orvak as reliable but frustrating. Since Anthea's death, Orvak has withdrawn further from wider politics, strengthened local militias and placed greater emphasis on regional unity.

> [!question] Rumour
> Some conspiratorial voices claim Orvak shelters sleeper agents or exiles involved in Anthea Bloodclot's assassination. The allegation is unproven and strongly denied by Orvaki elders.
