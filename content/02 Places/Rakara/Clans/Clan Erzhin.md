## Sigil

![[Clan Erzhin Sigil.png]]

The sigil of Clan Erzhin bears a **twin spiral on a cool stone-grey shield**. One spiral resembles etched stone and the other flowing wind. A chiseled mountain silhouette rises behind them.

- **Twin spirals:** stone and wind—enduring memory and evolving truth.
- **Mountain:** the clan's physical and spiritual foundation in the Sera Wilds.

## Background

Along the accessible foothills, mountain bases and outer valleys of the Sera Wilds, where wind howls through craggy corridors and clouds settle in cliffside hollows, Clan Erzhin endures.

The sealed interior of the range remains inaccessible. The Erzhini are a people of **rock and air**, carving both their homes and their memories into the bones of the world.

Their culture orbits two complementary pillars: **stone for what endures, and wind for what changes**. The clan's twin leaders embody that balance. **Talia** is a stern, calculating warrior; **Malen** is a charismatic and persuasive diplomat.

## People and culture

Erzhini settlements are often half-hidden, with homes and halls tunnelled into cliffs and mountains. Only minimal structures are visible from the surface.

The belief that **to forget the past is to weaken the present** shapes Erzhini society. Each family maintains a *Stonebook*: an ancestral and personal record carved from the mountain, passed down and expanded across generations.

The Erzhini are typically:

- Reserved and formal, speaking with careful purpose.
- Exceptionally educated, with reading and inscription taught from a young age.
- Deeply loyal to their clan and its record-keeping traditions.
- Fiercely proud of their cultural autonomy and precision of memory.

## Trade and tensions

Erzhin's close alignment with Minir causes concern elsewhere in Rakara. Centuries of trade, scholarship and diplomacy have made the clan a quiet beneficiary of Miniri wealth and technology.

- **Minir** regards Erzhin as a reliable partner in trade and academia.
- **Khaali and Voruun** are uneasy about Erzhin's perceived eastern tilt and the spread of Miniri ideals.
- **Orvak** maintains a distant but respectful relationship with Erzhin.

Following Anthea Bloodclot's assassination, Erzhin has remained calm and methodical, focusing on preserving accurate accounts.

> [!question] Rumour
> Some observers believe Erzhin might abandon Rakaran unity if Minir offered sufficient protection or incentive. No such agreement is publicly known.

## Faith

Wind and stone also shape Erzhini worship. The clan is particularly associated with [[01 Welcome to Vaelond/Religion/Rakaran Faiths#Ezaa of the Whispering Gale|Ezaa of the Whispering Gale]] and [[01 Welcome to Vaelond/Religion/Rakaran Faiths#Khurmog the Silent Deep|Khurmog the Silent Deep]].

## Known campaign site

[[02 Places/Rakara/Rana Viam|Rana Viam]], an Ardynian Chronarch ruin explored by the Defracturers, lies in the Erzhin hills. The party recovered the Bands of Distortion there after defeating a stone golem.
