## Sigil

![[Clan Temir Sigil.png]]

The sigil of Clan Temir shows a **crescent moon above ocean waves on a deep navy shield**, pierced by a downward-pointing white sword. Its clean lines reflect Temir's martial culture and belief in strength through discipline.

- **Moon:** the Moon-Father, guidance, discipline and timing in warfare.
- **Waves:** the Sea-Mother, providence, danger and survival through hardship.
- **Sword:** martial heritage and the belief that steel, not words, defines legacy.

## Background

Clan Temir is Rakara's most openly martial power—**fierce, unyielding and forged in salt and blood**. Its homeland spans rocky southern coasts, deep fjords and storm-battered bays lined with fishing villages and stone watchtowers. Temiri culture is built on endurance, discipline and the conviction that steel holds truth better than any tongue.

Temir's strategic ports and seaborne trade are as important as its warriors. Salted fish, dried kelp and shell-based alchemical goods flow through its harbours. Perfid merchants have historically used Temir as their preferred coastal stop when travelling towards Minir, circumventing Doro's inland controls and earning Temir both wealth and enemies.

Clan leaders are selected through demonstrated skill and campaign merit as much as bloodline. The current leader, **Avaz**, won acclaim by destroying a pirate keep off the Temiri coast.

## People and culture

The Temiri are pragmatic and proud, raised to the rhythms of the tide and disciplined from childhood. Brutal winters, storm-tossed seas and the constant strain of shipboard life have produced a culture of resilience, clarity and loyalty to kin. They have little patience for elaborate diplomacy but respect honour, commitment and deeds.

Their religion centres on the [[Rakaran Faiths#Sea-Mother and Moon-Father|Sea-Mother and Moon-Father]]. Common rites include:

- Moonlight duels to resolve disputes.
- Offerings of bone and salt cast into the sea before voyages.
- Coming-of-age trials in which young warriors dive from cliffs to recover relics placed by elders.

> [!quote]
> *“Nothing sacred is safe from the sea.”*

The saying reminds the Temiri that even greatness must survive testing.

## Trade and tensions

Temir's control over southern sea routes makes it indispensable to Perfid and Minir, but that influence is being challenged.

- **Perfid** relies on Temiri ports but regularly causes friction by pushing Elgiran religious influence into coastal settlements.
- **Minir** maintains a practical and respectful trading relationship, although its diplomats find Temir difficult to influence.
- **Doro** resents the routes that allow merchants to bypass its tolls and controls.
- **Voruun's rival port** is viewed as a direct challenge to Temiri coastal supremacy. Temiri warships now patrol closer to Voruun territory.

Temir remains powerful but increasingly restless. While the clan respects Anthea Bloodclot's memory, many Wavecutters are frustrated by the political stalemate and whisper that peace must be seized through strength rather than negotiated.

> [!question] Unconfirmed
> Several merchant convoys have disappeared amid the Temir–Voruun dispute. Neither clan publicly accepts responsibility.
