# The Rootbound

> *“Break the roots, free the forest.”*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Dissent within [[Clan Orvak]] |
| **Structure** | Independent cells with no accepted central command |
| **Symbol** | An inverted evergreen with chained roots |
| **Shared cause** | Resistance to imposed hierarchy and outside control |

## Symbol

![[Rootbound Sigil.png]]

The Rootbound use an **inverted evergreen whose roots terminate in chains**.

- The evergreen recalls Orvak's endurance.
- Its inversion rejects the idea that tradition must always be obeyed.
- The chains represent obligations to chiefs, foreign patrons and distant rulers.

The mark is carved into wood, stone and sometimes flesh. Depending on the cell, it may be a warning, a threat or a rallying sign.

## Origins

The movement emerged as Orvaki communities were pressed to accept stronger tribal leadership and greater participation in Anthea Bloodclot's project of clan unity. Many Orvaki considered this a practical response to a changing world. The Rootbound regarded it as the surrender of local autonomy.

Their central argument was simple: Orvak drew its strength from small, resilient communities bound by kinship and custom, not from obedience to a distant chieftain, a foreign patron or a self-appointed Lord of the Five Clans.

Anthea became the movement's defining enemy. Rootbound songs described her as a **“poisoned seed”**, an outsider whose alliances would reshape Orvak until little of its independence remained.

## One name, many movements

The Rootbound's contradictions are not separate discarded versions of the organisation. They are a consequence of its design.

Cells act independently and often pursue rival ambitions. They share symbols, coded songs and a broad hostility to imposed authority, but no leader can enforce a single interpretation of the creed. The movement's decentralisation protects it from destruction while repeatedly undermining its own message.

Some cells are **radical decentralists**. They welcome outsiders who accept local autonomy and imagine a network of self-governing communities linked by custom rather than law.

Other cells are **isolationist and exclusionary**, presenting themselves as guardians of Orvaki purity. They treat trade, migration and foreign alliances as cultural erosion.

Still others are primarily violent opportunists who use Rootbound language to justify sabotage, private vendettas or paid work. Two cells may use the same symbol while considering one another traitors.

## Methods

Actions attributed to Rootbound cells include:

- Sabotage of trade routes and foreign supply lines.
- Threats against leaders judged to be compromised.
- Poisoning and targeted assassination.
- Attacks on caravans, officials and political intermediaries.
- Use of fear to make cooperation with outsiders costly.

Because responsibility is deliberately fragmented, an act committed by one cell does not necessarily reflect the plans or approval of another.

## Anthea Bloodclot

The Rootbound have taken **quiet credit** for Anthea's death through private boasts, songs and coded claims, but the movement has never issued an open confirmation. Some cells call the killing an act of purification; others deny responsibility while celebrating the result.

The public case remains uncertain. The party's investigation has gone further.

> [!info] What the Defracturers uncovered
> The Defracturers found evidence that Perfid, acting through [[Alistair Prusset]], hired a Rootbound cell to kill Anthea Bloodclot. They have also directly observed Rootbound agents cooperating with Perfidian interests and found a Rootbound insignia among those responsible for the destruction of Buggie's gang.

## Perfid and the Rootbound

Perfid funds, shields or employs parts of the movement when convenient. This is a pragmatic relationship rather than a true ideological alliance. Rootbound rhetoric condemns foreign interference, yet individual cells accept foreign coin and protection when it advances their own goals.

That hypocrisy is not hidden by a central leadership because no such leadership exists. One cell may work with Perfid while another considers that cooperation proof of betrayal.
