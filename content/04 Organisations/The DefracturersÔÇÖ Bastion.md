# The Defracturers’ Bastion

The party’s headquarters occupies the former Perfid embassy in [[Somnolent]]. It was granted to the Defracturers after they entered Romayne Drayton’s patronage and assaulted the embassy to rescue Terrick Lowgins.

## Recorded facilities and work

The session chronicle mentions workshops, a library and a sanctuary. Initial assignments included preparing uniforms and researching the Loyal Crows, while the sanctuary was left idle.
