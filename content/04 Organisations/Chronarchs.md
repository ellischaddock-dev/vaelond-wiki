# Chronarchs

![[Chronarchs Symbol.png|240]]

The **Chronarchs** were Ardynian practitioners who meddled with time magic. The party’s encounters suggest their work combined extraordinary temporal effects with a willingness to spend lives as fuel.

## Campaign record

- Tace Nox contained a device that siphoned life energy from imprisoned hags to extend or restore the lives of others.
- Other sites have contained time stasis, resetting challenges, temporal residues known as Time Juice and relics associated with the Sundering.
- The motives, hierarchy and ultimate fate of the Chronarchs remain uncertain.
