# The Defracturers

The adventurers were informally called the **Squishy Boys** during their earliest exploits. They formally adopted the name **The Defracturers** when they accepted [[Romayne Drayton|Romayne Drayton’s]] patronage in Session 11.

## Members

- **Ballar Novis** — high elf paladin connected to Doro and House Nirrell.
- **Erelmor** — drow rogue pursuing those responsible for the destruction of his family.
- **Ieuan** — wood elf Circle of Stars druid with ties to Clan Orvak.
- **Enrak Kiran** — half-elf fire mage from Clan Khaali.
- **n’Dong** — dragonborn wizard and former captive of the Broken Fang.

## Formal status

Following Perfid’s actions in Somnolent, the group entered formal patronage under [[Romayne Drayton]] and became **The Defracturers**. They were granted resources, political protection and use of the former Perfid embassy as a bastion.

## Current campaign position

After Session 19, the party holds the Bands of Distortion and has emerged from Rana Viam into a confrontation with Broken Fang scouts, while their horses are reportedly in the hands of Ulsar Quinn.
