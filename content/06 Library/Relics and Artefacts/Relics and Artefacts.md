# Relics and Artefacts

## Sundering relics encountered

- [[Bands of Distortion]] — recovered from the golem at Rana Viam.
- [[Staff of Broken Time]] — briefly recovered in Tace Nox before the ruin reclaimed it.
- [[Corona of the Ashen Choir]] — carried by a Perfidian fanatic who escaped Somnolent.

These artefacts are connected to the Sundering of Ardynia. Their complete functions and the full set of ritual tools remain uncertain to the party.
