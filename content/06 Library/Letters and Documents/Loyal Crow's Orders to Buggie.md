# Loyal Crow’s Orders to Buggie

![[Loyal Crow's Orders to Buggie.png|500]]

## Transcription

> Buggie,
>
> The dour longshanks in silver — Prussett — is the one I want. Alive. Unharmed if possible. Scared if not. Take him under cover of chaos; the town will be distracted during Emberwake. Fire and panic are fine, but do not kill unless it becomes necessary. I have no interest in mourning mothers.
>
> The coins provided will buy you little. But will earn you shelter if the job turns messy. I will collect Elgira’s fawn once the heat has died down, with your full payment.
>
> He’s done things. Things that never saw trial, but Rakara will not forget his ministrations. Let’s make sure he gets the justice the courts could not provide.
>
> A’s loyal Crow

## Significance

The order directs Buggie to abduct Alistair Prusset during Emberwake while avoiding unnecessary civilian deaths. Later campaign notes identify the authoring Crow as [[Eden Carrion]].
