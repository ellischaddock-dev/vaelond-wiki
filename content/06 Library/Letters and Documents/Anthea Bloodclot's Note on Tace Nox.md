# Anthea Bloodclot’s Note on Tace Nox

![[Anthea Bloodclot's Note on Tace Nox.png|500]]

## Transcription

> To whatever poor fool reads this — allow me to save you some teeth-grinding.
>
> The lights must form an arrow. That’s the key. Point it at the altar, or you’ll be swarmed. You’ve got six moves. No more. Six.
>
> Why an arrow? Because of course the Ardynians — curse be their brittle, egotistical bones — could never pass up a chance to overcomplicate a situation. Gods forbid they just wrote: “Push here to not die.”
>
> I swear, every vault, every lock, every tomb of theirs is a shrine to smug superiority. Ageless? Yes. But if you outlive death and still need to solve three riddles and cast a starlight incantation to open your bloody wine cabinet, you haven’t conquered time — you’ve wasted it.
>
> I’ve already lost three crows to the damned thing, only Eden and I remain. If I ever find a chronarch that survived, if just one managed to evade Elgira’s clutches, I will put a knife through their eye and scatter their ashes across Rakara.
>
> A.B.

The source image also contains a flower-like mark and several columns of symbols. These have been preserved in the image but not interpreted.

## Significance

- Gives the solution to the six-move light puzzle in Tace Nox.
- Indicates that Anthea and Eden were the last two surviving Crows at that time.
- Shows Anthea’s hostility towards surviving Chronarchs and suggests Elgira acted against them.
