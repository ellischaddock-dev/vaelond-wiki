# Stolen Letter from Alistair Prusset’s Study

![[Letter from Arch-Illuminant Serathiel.png|500]]

## Transcription

> Ambassador Prussett,
>
> The High Council has reviewed your concerns regarding the Bloodclot woman. Her defiance is not merely political — it is theological trespass. Her elevation threatens the balance we have laboured to maintain.
>
> We concur that decisive action is now justified. You are advised to seek the rootbound. They operate beneath the canopy, but their reach extends far beyond it. They understand silence, and how to gift it.
>
> As ever, discretion is paramount.
>
> Arch-Illuminant Serathiel.

## Significance

The letter directly authorises decisive action against Anthea Bloodclot and instructs Prusset to contact the Rootbound.

> [!info] Church offices
> **Arch-Illuminant Serathiel** and **High Illuminant Derathel** are different people in different offices. Derathel is the head of the Church; Serathiel is the senior official who signed this instruction.
