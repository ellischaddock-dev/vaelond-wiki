# Broken Fang Contraband Manifest

> [!summary] At a glance
> A manifest recovered by n'Dong from a concealed Broken Fang drop site, identifying a supply network connecting locations across Rakara.

## Recovery

n'Dong discovered the manifest inside a shack used to store smuggled supplies near Somnolent's docks.

The site contained crates, whiskey and wrapped bricks of an unidentified substance. After sampling one of the bricks, n'Dong suffered severe pain, convulsions and temporary incapacity. The party later identified the substance on the manifest as **Agony**.

## Reconstructed transcription

The following entries are preserved in the Session 7 record:

| Destination | Recorded delivery |
|---|---|
| **One Ball Inn, Somnolent** | 4 × barrels of whiskey |
| **The Scythian Tree, Orvak** | 2 × rations; 4 × weapons or armour |
| **Fighting Pits, Voruun** | Agony; Powdered Power; Dragon's Dandruff |
| **Ashka's Den, Khaali** | Unicorn Wax; Ember Berries |

## Significance

The manifest shows that the [[04 Organisations/Broken Fang|Broken Fang]] operated a distribution network extending across several Rakaran territories.

It also established that:

- the [[03 People/Notable Figures/Antoinne Cassan|One Ball Inn]] was part of the supply route;
- weapons and armour were being moved towards Orvak;
- unusual substances were being delivered to the Voruun fighting pits;
- a separate Khaali contact or destination received rare materials;
- the Somnolent shipment was only one part of a wider operation.

The purpose and legality of each listed substance have not all been confirmed.
