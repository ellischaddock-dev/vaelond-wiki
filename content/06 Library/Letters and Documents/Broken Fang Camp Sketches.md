# Broken Fang Camp Sketches

> [!summary] At a glance
> Two crude maps drawn by the captured Broken Fang scouts Salla and Malt, showing the camp commanded by [[03 People/Notable Deceased/Ulsar Quinn|Ulsar Quinn]] and the approximate location of the Defracturers' stolen horses.

## Circumstances

During the pursuit outside [[02 Places/Rakara/Rana Viam|Rana Viam]], the Defracturers captured two fleeing Broken Fang scouts: **Salla** and **Malt**.

The party demanded that each scout draw a map of the nearby camp, placing particular emphasis on the location where the stolen horses were being held. The request was presented as a contest in which useful information might determine which prisoner survived.

Both scouts produced a sketch.

## Comparison and reliability

The two sketches were similar enough that the Defracturers considered their broad layout believable.

Information that can be treated with the greatest confidence:

- a Broken Fang camp exists nearby;
- it is associated with Ulsar Quinn;
- the Defracturers' horses are being held there;
- both prisoners described broadly the same arrangement.

Information that remains unreliable or incomplete:

- precise scale and distance;
- compass orientation;
- the number and positions of guards;
- patrol routes;
- fortifications, traps and hidden approaches;
- whether the camp layout changed after the scouts departed.

The sketches should be treated as reconnaissance aids rather than accurate surveyed maps.

## Current significance

The maps are the party's principal source of information for planning an infiltration or assault to recover:

- Kingcup;
- Bracken;
- Nail;
- Vesper;
- Cobb.

It is not yet known whether the camp has been warned about the missing scouts or the party's possession of the [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Bands of Distortion|Bands of Distortion]].
