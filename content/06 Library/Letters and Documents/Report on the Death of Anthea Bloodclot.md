## Record

On the morning of the [[Emberwake Festival]], Anthea Bloodclot was discovered dead inside her caravan, stationed approximately two miles east of [[Somnolent]] along the southern road. She had been scheduled to address the gathered people later that day. Her attendance had been promoted most strongly by [[Alistair Prusset]], envoy of Perfid, and [[Eden Carrion]], liaison of Clan Voruun.

The body was discovered by **Sable Greentree**, a local merchant employed by the town to deliver festival supplies.

## Known recent contacts

- **[[Callum Redmayne]]** saw Anthea on the evening before her death with Eden Carrion and **[[Summer Quaresh]]**, sharing drinks.
- **[[Elmwood Bluevine]]** reported that Anthea had visited him one week earlier seeking information about a venture into Thistlewound.
- **[[Ersil Loquella]]** spoke with her two days before her death while gathering material for a ballad.

## Cause of death

Initial examination suggested natural causes. **[[Father Zantos]]**, Elder Druid of Somnolent, identified signs consistent with poisoning by **Bloodspore**, a rare and potent herb cultivated exclusively in Clan Orvak's lands.

The poison was probably administered in the wine found inside the caravan, which remained half-consumed.

## Loss of the crime scene

No comprehensive inquiry was conducted by the town guard. During the Emberwake festivities, the caravan and all evidence within it were removed by unknown parties. This prevented preservation of the scene and any later complete examination.

## Suspected interests

The report records several possible motives without assigning guilt:

- The **Rootbound** possessed access to Bloodspore and had openly opposed Anthea, but may have lacked the wealth and logistics to act alone.
- **Somnolent** resisted incorporation into Anthea's project, and [[Romayne Drayton]] had publicly opposed attempts to force the town into the fold.
- **Doro** feared the trade and military consequences of a unified Rakara.
- **Perfid** had condemned Anthea's interest in Ardynian history as heretical, despite Prusset publicly championing her Emberwake appearance.
- **Minir** was rumoured to have supported some of Anthea's early activity before becoming quieter as her power grew.

## Later significance

The report established Bloodspore poisoning and the removal of the caravan as central facts in the party's investigation. Later evidence connected Perfid, Alistair Prusset and a Rootbound cell to the murder; those conclusions were not available when this document was entered into Somnolent's records.
