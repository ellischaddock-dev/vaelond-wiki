# Open Threads

> [!info] Review basis
> This tracker has been reconciled against the chronicle through Session 19. **New** threads emerged recently, **active** threads still drive the campaign, **reframed** threads remain relevant but no longer in their original form, and **stale** threads have seen no meaningful progress for many sessions.
