# Horses

The party's five mounts, as recorded in the campaign tracker and Session 15. The rider assignments below are the confirmed repository canon.

## Bracken

![[Bracken.png|260]]

**Recorded rider:** [[03 People/Player Characters/Enrak Kiran|Enrak Kiran]]

**D&D Beyond sheet:** [Bracken](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6298820-bracken)

Slow but exceptionally steady. Bracken stood against an owlbear to protect Enrak.

## Cobb

![[Cobb.png|260]]

**Recorded rider:** [[03 People/Player Characters/Ieuan|Ieuan]]

**D&D Beyond sheet:** [Cobb](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6298828-cobb)

A dependable, calm and sturdy mount. The name later caused some amusement when the party met a farmer also called Cobb.

## Vesper

![[Vesper.png|260]]

**Recorded rider:** [[03 People/Player Characters/Erelmor|Erelmor]]

**D&D Beyond sheet:** [Vesper](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6298790-vesper)

An elegant dark horse suited to stealth and grace. Vesper fled the scorpion battle carrying an unconscious Erelmor to safety.

## Nail

![[Nail.png|260]]

**Recorded rider:** [[03 People/Player Characters/n'Dong|n'Dong]]

**D&D Beyond sheet:** [Nail](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6298832-nail)

A former courier horse: quick, enduring and somewhat shy when battle approaches.

## Kingcup

![[Kingcup.png|260]]

**Recorded rider:** [[03 People/Player Characters/Ballar Novis|Ballar Novis]]

**D&D Beyond sheet:** [Kingcup](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6298836-kingcup)

Beautiful, vain, difficult and more suited to a parade ground than a battlefield; an immediate match for Ballar.

## Current situation

At the end of [[05 Campaign Chronicle/Session 21 - Contracts, Command, and a Burning Camp|Session 21]]:

- The horses are no longer held by Ulsar Quinn's forces.
- Ulsar's Broken Fang camp near Rana Viam was destroyed.
- Bracken was threatened by Talan Rook during the parley, when Talan pressed a greatsword to his neck.
- Erelmor began freeing Nail during the infiltration, cutting two of the three ropes holding him.
- Kingcup was kicked by a warhorse during the chaos and nearly died.
- Mireya, who had been watching the horses, was killed in the burning camp and preserved by Ballar with Gentle Repose.
