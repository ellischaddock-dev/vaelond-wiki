# Enrak Kiran

> [!summary] At a glance
> A half-elf fire mage from Clan Khaali whose ambitions, ancient book and determination to solve Anthea Bloodclot's murder have drawn him into the search for Ardynian secrets.

## Established background

- Enrak is associated with [[02 Places/Rakara/Clans/Clan Khaali|Clan Khaali]].
- His magic is strongly focused on fire.
- He possesses an ancient book whose full significance has not yet been established.
- He has expressed a desire to become Khaali's foremost fire mage, linking that ambition to solving Anthea's murder.

## Campaign role

Enrak provides destructive and utility magic, with fire serving as his most recognisable approach to danger. His curiosity about ancient material has made him a natural participant in the exploration of Tace Nox and [[02 Places/Rakara/Rana Viam|Rana Viam]].

During Session 20, Enrak pursued Salla alongside Ballar, resisted the bog siren's song and was webbed during the giant-spider attack before helping to defeat the creatures.

## Mount

[[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Horses#Bracken|Bracken]] is Enrak's recorded mount. Bracken is slow but exceptionally steady and once stood against an owlbear to protect him.

- Solve the remaining questions surrounding Anthea's murder.
- Establish what recognition or office he is seeking within Clan Khaali.
- Understand the relationship between his fire magic and the relics or ruins now being uncovered.
