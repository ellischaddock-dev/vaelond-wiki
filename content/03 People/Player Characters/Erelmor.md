# Erelmor

> [!summary] At a glance
> A drow rogue and survivor of Ashenhom whose search for those responsible for the deaths of his wife and children has made revenge one of the Defracturers' longest-running personal investigations.

## Established background

- Erelmor survived the destruction of **Ashenhom**.
- His wife and children were killed there.
- He has past knowledge of, or contacts among, the [[04 Organisations/Rootbound|Rootbound]].
- His central personal objective is to identify and punish those responsible for his family's deaths.

## Campaign role

Erelmor is the party's scout, infiltrator and most direct practitioner of covert violence. His experience with the Rootbound and the history of Ashenhom frequently place him at the centre of investigations involving Doro, House Nirrell and the Old Grey Pike.

His sleep has repeatedly been disturbed by nightmares and by a song connected to **the Pike**. The party's investigation established that [[03 People/Notable Figures/Ervain Megbanas|Ervain Megbanas]] is the Old Grey Pike, but did not resolve the full Ashenhom conspiracy.

Erelmor can establish psychic communication between separated allies. During Session 20, he used this ability to keep the divided pursuit groups in contact. He also killed the restrained first scout before the party could interrogate him.

## Mount

[[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Horses#Vesper|Vesper]] is Erelmor's recorded mount. Vesper previously carried an unconscious Erelmor away from danger during the scorpion battle.

## Active personal threads

- Identify the people and institutions responsible for Ashenhom.
- Determine the Old Grey Pike's true role in those events.
- Reconcile Erelmor's former Rootbound associations with the movement's involvement in Anthea's murder.
- Decide how far he is willing to go in pursuit of revenge.

> - His precise role and life in Ashenhom before its destruction remain unclear.
> - The nature and duration of his past Rootbound involvement are only partially established.
> - The source and meaning of the song and nightmares connected to the Pike remain unresolved.
