# Ieuan

> [!summary] At a glance
> A wood-elf Circle of Stars druid from Clan Orvak whose reverence for the natural world is matched by an alarming willingness to speak with, bargain with and occasionally cultivate its more dangerous inhabitants.

## Established background

- Ieuan is from [[02 Places/Rakara/Clans/Clan Orvak|Clan Orvak]] and was regarded as a gifted young druid.
- [[03 People/Notable Figures/Father Zantos|Father Zantos]] is an important mentor.
- His beliefs particularly involve **Orun the Verdant Kin**, **Ezaa of the Whispering Gale** and **Khurmog the Silent Deep**.
- His sister **Deyna** is connected to his unresolved history with Orvak and the Rootbound.

## Campaign role

Ieuan serves as the party's wilderness guide, healer and interpreter of natural or magical phenomena. His transformations and tracking have repeatedly enabled the group to travel or pursue targets through difficult terrain.

Father Zantos entrusted him with a [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Star Seed|Star Seed]]. During the rest inside [[02 Places/Rakara/Rana Viam|Rana Viam]], the seed produced a leaf bearing the constellation associated with Ieuan's Starry Form.

In Session 20, Ieuan used **Speak with Plants** to communicate with [[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Plague|Plague]], a sentient poisonous fungus. He promised to spread five cuttings in exchange for safe passage and planted the first in Salla's corpse. His later proposal to use Plague against the Broken Fang camp was designated **Plan E**.

## Mount

[[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Horses#Cobb|Cobb]] is Ieuan's recorded mount: dependable, calm and sturdy.
