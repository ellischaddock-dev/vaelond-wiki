# Ballar Novis

> [!summary] At a glance
> A proud and conspicuous high-elf paladin from Doro whose family connections, appetite for heroics and unusual ring have drawn him into several of the campaign's deepest mysteries.

## Established background

- Ballar is connected to **House Nirrell** in Doro.
- His parents, Lucinda and Blodren, have links to Doroan diplomacy and the history surrounding the Old Grey Pike.
- He is characteristically bold, proud and keen to be seen acting heroically.
- His paladin oath is associated with glory and the pursuit of memorable deeds.

## Campaign role

Ballar often acts as the party's visible champion and negotiator, though his confidence can produce unconventional solutions.

He has fought at the centre of many of the party's most dangerous encounters, helped capture Prusset and repeatedly restored fallen companions.

He carries a [[06 Library/Relics and Artefacts/Ring of Mind Shielding|Ring of Mind Shielding]] containing a speaking consciousness known to the party as [[03 People/Notable Figures/Din|Din]]. Din has offered guidance in exchange for the promise of a body, but his identity and motives remain unresolved.

During the pursuit in Session 20, Ballar captured Salla, placed him in the bag of holding and later revived n'Dong during the Egg Hunter attack.

## Mount

[[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Horses#Kingcup|Kingcup]] is Ballar's recorded mount: beautiful, vain, difficult and well matched to his rider.
