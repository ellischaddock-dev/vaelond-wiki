# Karessa

Karessa is a Khaali woman named by former Dazuun fighters [[03 People/Mentioned Figures/Yama and Spopovich|Yama and Spopovich]] as their supplier of [[06 Library/Campaign Reference/Dragon's Dandruff|Dragon's Dandruff]]. The fighters described her as having **three fingers**, potentially making her recognisable if their account is reliable.

The Defracturers have not met her or verified the allegation. It is particularly important **not to identify her as one of the three Khaali alchemists** said by Ulsar Quinn to manufacture the stimulant south of Somnolent: that connection remains unproven. Her location, methods and ties to Rhul Slowdrift are unknown.
