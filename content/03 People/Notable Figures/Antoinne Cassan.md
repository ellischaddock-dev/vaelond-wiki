# Antoinne Cassan

![[Antoinne Cassan.png|260]]

> [!summary] At a glance
> The unwelcoming landlord of the One Ball Inn and a participant in the Broken Fang’s stolen-goods network.

## Known details

- Runs the One Ball Inn and is notably inhospitable for a landlord.
- Works with the Broken Fang to buy stolen goods.
- Suspects n’Dong of interfering with the operation.
