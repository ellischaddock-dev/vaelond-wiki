# Gayle Bramble

![[Gayle Bramble.png|260]]

Gayle Bramble is one of Somnolent's senior civic authorities, serving as a deputy whose responsibilities involve the town's security and investigations. Her work has repeatedly overlapped with the Defracturers' search for the people behind Anthea Bloodclot's death.

## Investigation beyond Somnolent

During the adventurers' absence, the Bastion researchers developed a lead suggesting that missing Loyal Crows [[03 People/Notable Figures/Eden Carrion|Eden Carrion]] and [[03 People/Notable Figures/Summer Quaresh|Summer Quaresh]] might have been seen in a region with naturally dampened magic. Gayle and [[03 People/Notable Figures/Job Ashmere|Job Ashmere]] prepared to investigate and subsequently departed. Their discoveries and the full course of the mission have not yet been recorded.

## Her injured return

At the end of the most recent recorded session, Gayle returned alone to the Defracturers' Bastion and collapsed unconscious and badly injured at the entrance as Joe-Seff Gorbels opened the door. **She is not recorded as dead**, and Job's absence does not establish his fate. What attacked or injured her, and whether either investigator found Eden or Summer, remains unknown.
